Linking two devices by QR

Device A on :8103, device B on :8104 — two origins, so two real device keys and two logs. A draws its pairing offer as a QR; B reads that QR off the canvas and pairs from what it read. Nothing is handed between them but pixels. Then a delta authored on A has to reach B through the relay on :8787, and the SAS words each device shows for the other have to be the ones the CORE derives — computed here by core_wasm.wasm, not by JavaScript that agrees with itself.
