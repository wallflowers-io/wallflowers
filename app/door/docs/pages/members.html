A role applies only to someone already on the roster. A title grants nothing. Show a button only to someone who may press it: the owner is the object's owner, admins are in roles.

To list the members, with what each says about themselves, their answers, and the community's questions, read GET /v2/members.

Becoming a member

Your site's session reaches the community only when the person signed in is one of its members; for anyone else, /v2/graph has no community, and the starter says so. A person joins with a claim from your community's QR code: POST /v2/join, from your site's own session. Adding and removing members by hand, and invitations, are done in WallFlowers: a site's session is refused on /v2/add. {{MEMBERS_ADD}}

Order people as WallFlowers does: the owner, then admins, then everyone else.
