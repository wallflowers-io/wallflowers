Register your app

Before a member can sign in on your site, WallFlowers registers it: which community it shows, where its pages are, and where sign-in returns to.

{{DEV}}

1. Make your community

Sign up at wallflowers.io/signup: choose what it is, its name and its address, {{SIGNUP_APP_STEP}}then your account. {{SIGNUP_NOTE}} You land in WallFlowers' app, the community's owner.{{SIGNUP_APP_ID}} To find its id: while signed in, open {{DOOR}}/v2/graph in a new tab; find the object with "kind": "group" and your community's name; its id is the id beside it. (Its public page, "kind": "host", has the same name: not that one.) If it answers not signed in, sign in to WallFlowers in that browser and reload the tab. Once a member signs in on your site, the same id is session.site.

2. Send your registration

FieldExample
community71c1…6a6cThe community your site shows (its id).

originshttp://localhost:5173Where your pages are served from. List every one: production, a preview, each local port. localhost and 127.0.0.1 are different origins.
home (optional)https://your.site/communityOnly if your community hands out join QR codes (a kiosk): where WallFlowers sends a scanned one, as #join=… (POST /v2/join). On one of your origins.
{{REGISTER}}

WallFlowers gives you back your client id. A session from your site reaches that community and nothing else.

{{TEAMS}}

A wrong callback is refused with a 400 that begins "<callback>" is not a callback client "<client>" registered, and an unknown client id with one that begins no client "<client>" is registered. {{REG_TIMING}}
