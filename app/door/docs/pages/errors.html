Errors

A refusal is an HTTP status and a sentence from WallFlowers, as plain text in the body (await res.text()), not JSON; only a batch's 422 is JSON. There are no error codes, and the wording is not a contract: show the sentence, don't parse it. Each route's own refusals are on Routes.

StatusMeansDo
401, or 400 not signed inNot signed in, or the session ended.Sign in again: show the button, or send them once by yourself, never in a loop (The session).
400 "… is outside this site's scope"That object isn't in your session's reach.Don't offer it.
400 · 403, otherNot allowed, a malformed argument, or a route that isn't for sites.Show the sentence.
A network error, no statusThe browser stopped a call from a page origin that isn't registered (CORS).Serve from a registered origin (Your page's rules).
404 "no such page"No published page at that address (public page routes).Check the address.
413The body is over {{BODY}}.Send less.
422A batch stopped part-way: { made, refused: { step, why } }.Keep what was made; retry only the rest.
429 "too many sign-ins are open from here"{{PER_ADDR}} sign-ins already in progress from your address.Finish or cancel one, or wait a minute.
503 "the Door is at its session limit"As many sessions are open as WallFlowers holds, most of them left to idle out.Sign out every session you open; try again shortly.
502 · 503 · no answerBusy or unreachable.Try again shortly.
