WallFlowers Community API

Build your own site or app on a WallFlowers community. Members sign in with their passkey in WallFlowers' window; your page then reads the community's rooms and people and writes as that member.

New here? The Quickstart gets a working page, signed in and posting to a room, in about ten minutes. AI coding agents: read agents.md.

How it works

  • A community is what a person makes when they sign up at wallflowers.io: its rooms, its members, its public page. Your site is registered to one community. The API calls it the Site (session.site is its id), and the model calls its kind group: one thing, three names.
  • The Door is WallFlowers' API and sign-in server, {{DOOR}}. Signing in happens in its window, with the member's passkey. Your page never sees a password, a passkey or a key. It gets a session.
  • Every call is session.fetch('/v2/…') as that member: JSON in, JSON out. Read everything at once with GET /v2/graph; write one change with POST /v2/apply; hear about changes with session.events.
  • What you may do is decided by the data model, which the Door serves at /v2/icd: every kind of object, every op, its arguments, and who may write it.
  • The public page (events, resources, pictures) is served by {{ARC}} and readable by anyone at /v1/face/<address>, no session{{PUBLISHED_INDEX}}. The address is the community's wallflowers.io/<address>.
