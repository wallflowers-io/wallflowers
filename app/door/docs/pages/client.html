A client that holds up

The habits of a page that stays right with many people in it.

  1. One change stream per page, shared by everything on it: session.events once, and fan out.
  2. Coalesce: re-read /v2/graph 300 ms after the last changed. A busy room changes several times a second.
  3. A slow net: while a room is open and the tab visible, re-read every 15 s whatever the stream says. It covers a stream that died quietly.
  4. After your own write, re-read at once, so the member sees their post.
  5. A new account settles: it can read as "not a member" for a few seconds. Re-read every 2 s for up to 20 s before saying so.
  6. A late joiner may see no rooms until the owner's app has admitted them. Re-read every 1.5 s for up to 15 s.
  7. On a 401, show the Sign in button again. If you send them to sign in yourself, at most once per page view, never in a loop.
  8. Show refusals: a folds: false object as unavailable, with its why; a refused write with WallFlowers' sentence.
  9. Names: from profiles, else "New member". Never a key.
  10. Load the sign-in script once, pinned. If it fails to load, let the next attempt try again.
  11. Text only: draw members' words with textContent, never innerHTML, and make a member's link a link only if it starts with http: or https:.
