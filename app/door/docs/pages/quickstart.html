Quickstart

A page that signs a member in, lists the community's rooms, and posts to one. Two files, no build step, no dependencies.

Before you start, at the hackathon:

  • Sign out every session you open: {{SESSIONS}} sessions are held for everyone together.
  • Everyone on the venue's Wi-Fi shares one address, and {{PER_ADDR}} sign-ins can be in progress per address. 429 "too many sign-ins are open from here" means: finish or cancel yours, or wait a minute.
  • Test in a desktop browser on localhost. A phone can't reach your laptop's page: a LAN address isn't a secure context, and the sign-in needs one.
  • Keep your computer's clock right: a proof more than {{SKEW}} s off WallFlowers' clock is refused.

2. Download the starter

index.html and app.js. Put them in one folder, or from a terminal:

curl -O {{SITE}}/starter/index.html -O {{SITE}}/starter/app.js

At the top of app.js, set client to {{QS_CLIENT}}. The fields the starter reads from /v2/graph are explained on What you read.

export const CONFIG = {
  door: '{{DOOR}}',
  integrity: '{{SRI}}',
  client: '{{QS_CLIENT_JS}}',
  callback: location.origin + location.pathname,
};

3. Serve it

python3 -m http.server {{QS_PORT}} --bind 127.0.0.1

On Windows, py -m http.server {{QS_PORT}} --bind 127.0.0.1. With Node, npx http-server -p {{QS_PORT}} -a 127.0.0.1. Open the page at its address, not by double-clicking index.html: as a file it cannot run.

--bind 127.0.0.1 keeps the server to this machine. The address you open is still exactly http://localhost:{{QS_PORT}}/: not 127.0.0.1, and not /index.html. The callback is the page's own address and is matched exactly. Press Sign in: WallFlowers' window asks for the member's passkey and brings them back. The page lists the community's rooms; pick one and post. A new community has no rooms yet: in WallFlowers, open Rooms, then New room; or, as its owner, press New room in the starter (it opens one as Rooms and threads does).

Sign in as a member. The page reaches the community only for its members: at first, sign in with the account that owns it. Any other account gets, after a few seconds, "This account isn't a member of this community" (Becoming a member).

Set your name. Pages show people by the name on their card. Until you set yours in WallFlowers (open {{DOOR}}/#you, or the profile menu, then Your card), you appear as "New member" on every page, yours and everyone else's.

Sign out every session you open. Call session.signOut() (POST /v2/signout) when you're done, and have scripts and tests sign out as they exit. Otherwise WallFlowers holds the session until it has been idle for {{IDLE}}, and once it holds {{SESSIONS}} sessions, everyone's sign-in is refused with 503 "the Door is at its session limit; try again shortly", yours and every other team's.

4. What it does

  1. Loads the sign-in script once, pinned by its integrity hash.
  2. On load: WallFlowers.finish if the address carries ?state (the member is back from WallFlowers), otherwise WallFlowers.current. null means signed out.
  3. Reads GET /v2/graph, finds the community (session.site), and lists its parts with role room, in order, naming people from profiles.
  4. Posts with POST /v2/apply {object: roomId, op: 'forum.post', args: {text, ts}}, then reads the graph again.
  5. Opens one change stream and re-reads the graph 300 ms after the last change, and every 15 s while the page is visible.

If it doesn't work

The script doesn't load (the console says CORS)Your page's address isn't allowed yet: {{QS_ORIGIN_FIX}}. WallFlowers serves the sign-in script only to the addresses it knows.
The script doesn't load (the console says the digest didn't match)The integrity hash is wrong, or you loaded it from somewhere else. Use the one above.
… is not a callback client … registeredYour callback isn't registered character for character. Check the port, localhost against 127.0.0.1, and the trailing slash.
"This session reaches no community yet", or no rooms, for a few secondsNormal for a new account or a new member: the starter reads again for up to 20 s (no community) or 15 s (no rooms).
503 the Door is at its session limitToo many sessions are open, many left to idle out. Sign out what you open (session.signOut()), and try again shortly.
429 too many sign-ins are open from hereFinish or cancel an open sign-in, or wait a minute. At most {{PER_ADDR}} sign-ins can be in progress from one address.
